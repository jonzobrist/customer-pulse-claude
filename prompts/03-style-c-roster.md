# Agent brief 03 — Style C: Roster Board (executives)

You are building **Style C** of Customer Pulse: a draft-board style portfolio view for executives and senior leaders. It answers, in ten seconds, *which important customers are at risk, how much revenue is exposed, and what is being done*. It must feel like a team roster, not a report.

Read `prompts/README.md` (house rules) first. The foundation (brief 00) is merged: use `PulseCard`, `selectPortfolio`, the theme and the tokens.

**Visual spec:** `design/project/StyleC_Roster.dc.html`.

## Build (only in `app/src/views/roster/`)

1. **KPI strip:** ARR at risk (with today's change), Sev1 open, live incidents, renewals ≤ 90d, portfolio pulse, plus an ARR-by-status stacked bar with a text legend and an `aria-label` that summarizes it in words.
2. **Filters:** All · At risk · Renewing ≤ 90d · In incident, as real toggle buttons with counts and `aria-pressed`, reflected in the URL query so views can be shared.
3. **Roster grid:** 4 columns at ≥ 1280px, 2 at ≥ 720px, 1 below. Each roster card shows the status chip, flags (LIVE, S1, R90), name, tier · ARR · renewal, a big pulse number with delta, a pulse bar, and a one-line "why".
   - The **why line** is generated by a pure, tested function from the `CardModel` (template rules, no LLM yet). Examples are in the artboard's `renderVals()`.
   - Default sort: verdict band (critical → healthy), then pulse ascending, then ARR descending. Alternatives: risk-weighted ARR (`ARR × (100 − pulse)/100`), renewal soonest, ARR.
4. **Drill:** click → L1 card in a side sheet with "Open dossier" and "Message account team" actions. The message action is a stub that opens a prefilled draft dialog; never auto-send.
5. **Exec digest:** `/roster/digest` is a print-friendly one-pager (A4/Letter, works in light theme) of the top 10 at-risk accounts. This is what gets emailed each morning; agent 05 schedules it.
6. **Density toggle:** comfortable (artboard) vs compact (L0 chips, 8 columns) for 50+ accounts.

## Acceptance

- With the fixture under the default sort, the roster order matches the artboard: Halcyon Freight, Atlas Retail, Copperline Bank, Meridian Health, Northgate Media, Ferro Mobility, Bluepeak Energy, Quill & Vale, Solano Insurance, … A test asserts it.
- The why-line function has a unit test for every fixture account.
- KPI numbers are derived, not hard-coded: a test recomputes them from the fixture.
- Playwright screenshots: default, each filter, compact density, digest print preview, each in both themes, at 1440 and 390.

## Not in scope

Real Salesforce data (agent 06), scheduling the digest email (agent 05), and editing CRM fields.
